NERIA / native 64 x 64 study

Inspected source.png and four enlarged crops of reference_pixel.png using
Aseprite Lua, before drawing. Reference characters are not copied.

Reference observations:
- Pale-haired figures use a broad, almost flat ivory highlight, a cool middle
  tone and blue-violet shadow wedges; individual strands are rarely outlined.
- Head occupies roughly two fifths to half the figure. A small chin, compact
  torso and separated short legs keep the silhouettes readable at native size.
- Eyes are vertical colour blocks under horizontal dark upper lashes, with
  tiny light accents. Face shading is warm; cheek accents sit below the eyes.
- Outlines follow stepped diagonals with runs of 2-3 pixels; pale illuminated
  hair edges use a lighter outline than the dark costume and lower silhouette.
- Clothing reads through a few bold adjacent colour clusters, then very sparse
  trim. No continuous-tone gradients, dithering or random texture are needed.
- Accessories have a strong outer shape. Their interior detail is subordinate
  to that shape, especially at 1x.

Adaptation from the original illustration:
- Serene blue-grey eyes; closed tiny mouth, quiet eyebrows, slight right turn.
- White hair falls vertically behind the shoulders, with aqua only at its ends.
- Oversized white/navy keeper gloves at the waist; no ball or extra prop.
- High navy collar, diagonal shoulder straps, warm-white sleeves, navy shorts.
- Long asymmetrical white side panel carrying a small connected blue wave.
- White/navy socks and cleats; blue hair flower/tassel and a coral waist tassel.
- Light from upper left. Flat palette clusters, all pixels alpha 0 or 255.
- Requested 1px eye glints and minute decorative marks are intentional detail;
  'no isolated pixels' is checked as no detached foreground pixels/components.

The displayed reference sheet itself is scaled/softened; our output is drawn
at its native 64px grid and enlarged only with exact integer nearest-neighbour.
