The following material anchors are actual source.png pixels. Within each
material region the closest pixel to the visually selected source tone is
recorded. Coordinates are zero-based. The sprite ramps deliberately increase
contrast and brighten lights for legibility at 64 x 64. These are not literal
copies of every intermediate illustration shade. No colours are dithered.
