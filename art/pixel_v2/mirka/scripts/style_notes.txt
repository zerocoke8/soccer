Reference study: reference_pixel.png, four crops in reference_study.png.
The two dark cat-eared figures use head-plus-hair around 55-60% of their
visible height; the lavender-bob and blue-haired figures are around 50-55%.
Mirka target: 50 px high, ears/bob/face about 28 px, petite body about 22 px.
3/4 front: both eyes visible; face is offset right inside the hair silhouette.
Eyes: dark upper lash, a compact 2-3 px vertical iris, pale one-pixel glint.
Near eye slightly wider. Small peach cheek accents and 1-3 px mouth.
Hair: continuous broad midtone mass, 3-4 tones, highlight in connected band;
chunky 2-5 px locks rather than many individually outlined hair strands.
Outlines are deep plum/grey-purple/olive rather than black; generally 1 px
at native resolution. Broken/lightened edges on the upper-left are possible.
Clothes: broad light and dark blocks, 2-4 px sleeve clusters, short 2-4 px legs,
small feet. Jewellery simplified to a few gold pixels. Avoid tiny patterns.
Reference raster has scaling/compression artefacts; do not inherit those.
Final: 64x64, <=32 opaque colours, binary alpha, y=61 baseline, no ball.
